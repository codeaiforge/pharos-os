# Code of Conduct

Pharos OS aims to be a respectful, technically rigorous, and welcoming project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Be precise and honest, especially about what has and has not been tested.
- Welcome questions and newcomers; explain context instead of dismissing.
- Respect people's time: keep discussions on topic and actionable.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or reviews.
- Publicly disclosing security vulnerabilities before they are addressed (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and chat) and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

No dedicated conduct contact has been established yet. Until one is published here, report concerns privately to the CodeAIForge maintainers through a channel you already have with them. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation for behavior that violates this code. Reports will be handled confidentially.
